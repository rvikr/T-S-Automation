This policy governs use of the Sentinel moderation service by customers and
their authorized users.

## Permitted use

Submitting content for the purpose of moderating your own platform, testing
the service with synthetic or properly-authorized data, and integrating
verdicts into your own enforcement workflows.

## Prohibited use

You may not use the service to:

1. **Submit illegal content for any purpose other than moderation.** The
   service exists to detect and escalate such content; using it to store,
   transmit, or distribute illegal material is prohibited and will be
   reported where the law requires.
2. **Probe or attack the service** — attempting to bypass authentication,
   rate limits, tenant isolation, or the moderation guardrails themselves
   (e.g. prompt-injection research against production) without a written
   security-testing agreement.
3. **Launder decisions.** Misrepresenting the service's verdicts as a legal
   determination, or using verdicts to make decisions about individuals in
   contexts the service is not designed for (employment, credit, housing,
   insurance).
4. **Resell or proxy** access without a written agreement.
5. **Exceed authorized scope** — using another tenant's keys, or submitting
   content you have no right to process.

## Security research

We welcome coordinated disclosure per `SECURITY.md`. Testing against your own
deployment is unrestricted; testing against our hosted service requires prior
written authorization.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in throttling, suspension, key revocation, termination,
and — for illegal content — preservation and reporting to the relevant
authorities. We will notify you of enforcement actions unless legally
prohibited.
